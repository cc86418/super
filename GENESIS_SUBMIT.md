## Pitch

**Wheel Run** is a dark endless survival race where the live Squeekchain hamster wheel drives the world: `ACTIVE` RPM/speed raises scroll rate, hazard density, and score multiplier; `IDLE` slows the burrow; each on-chain `settledThroughRound` bump triggers a soft settlement quake. Polls `https://api.squeekchain.com/v1/status` ~1.5s. Real game, not a dashboard.
